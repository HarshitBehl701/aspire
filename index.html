The Services We Offer:

SAP Training Services

With over 20 years of experience supporting 22 SAP implementations across 80 countries, Diamond Learning Solutions delivers bespoke SAP training that drives successful user adoption and business transformation. We design and deliver end-user training, super user programmes, train-the-trainer workshops, go-live support and digital learning solutions across SAP S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, Fiori, FI/CO, MM, BW/BI, Concur, Workforce Software and other SAP technologies, helping organisations maximise the return on their SAP investment.

Corporate Training Consultancy

Our corporate training consultancy services help organisations develop learning strategies that align with business objectives and deliver measurable results. From training needs analysis and learningstrategy to change management, stakeholder engagement and digital transformation support, we work as an extension of your team to create practical, scalable learning solutions that improve performance, accelerate adoption and build long-term capability.

Mobile Learning Solutions

Modern learners need flexible access to training wherever they are. Our mobile learning solutions are designed for today's digital workforce, delivering responsive, bite-sized learning experiences that work seamlessly across smartphones, tablets and desktops. Whether supporting SAP transformations, compliance training or leadership development, we create accessible, engaging content that enables learning at the point of need and encourages continuous professional development.

Train-the-Trainer Programmes

Our Train-the-Trainer programmes develop the skills, confidence and capability of internal trainers, subject matter experts and business champions to deliver engaging and effective learning. Drawing on extensive experience supporting global SAP and business transformation programmes, we provide practical facilitation techniques, instructional design principles and coaching skills that ensure knowledge is transferred consistently across your organisation.

e-Learning Development in London

We create bespoke eLearning solutions that engage learners, improve knowledge retention and support business performance. Using industry-leading authoring tools including Articulate 360, Rise, Adobe Captivate and SAP Enable Now, we develop interactive SCORM and xAPI compliant learning, software simulations, scenario-based learning, compliance training and blended learning programmes that integrate seamlessly with your Learning Management System.

Video Learning Production

High-quality learning videos are one of the most effective ways to engage today's workforce. We produce professional training videos including software demonstrations, screen-recorded tutorials, animated explainers, instructor-led presentations and video-enhanced eLearning that simplify complex processes and provide learners with engaging, on-demand resources that can be accessed anytime, anywhere.

What We Believe: Our Core Values

  • Partnership Through Collaboration- We believe the best results come from working alongside our clients, not simply for them. We focus on improving user adoption, increasing confidence, enhancing performance and supporting successful organisational change. Every solution we deliver is designed to provide lasting value for both learners and the organisations they support.
  • Integrity and Trust- Honesty, transparency and accountability are at the heart of everything we do. We build long-term relationships through open communication, expert guidance and a commitment to delivering on our promises.
