What Makes an Effective Trainer?

The best trainers combine three important qualities:

Knowledge

They understand their subject and can share expertise clearly.

Connection

They build trust and create an environment where people feel comfortable learning.

Facilitation

Facilitation skills training help learners think, practise and apply new skills.
Our Train the Trainer programmes develop all three.
